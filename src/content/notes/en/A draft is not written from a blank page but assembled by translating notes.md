Writing from a blank page is daunting. In a Zettelkasten, drafting is not creation but **translation** — you pick out permanent notes you have already written and transplant them into a coherent piece aimed at a single claim. The thinking is already done at the note stage. What remains for the draft is arrangement and connection.

Two preconditions make this translation possible:
- The notes must already be written in my own words — [[Meaning sinks in only when a note is rewritten in my own words, not copied]]
- What to write is decided by the notes that have gathered — [[Topics are not decided from the top but emerge bottom-up from the notes that pile up]]
