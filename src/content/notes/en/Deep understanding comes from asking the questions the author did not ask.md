To understand more deeply, you cannot stay trapped inside the frame of the sentence.

You have to think of the questions the author never got around to asking the reader. To understand a sentence, do not stop at accepting what is written on the surface. Ask yourself "why is that", "how far does this apply", and "is there no opposite case". Understanding stops where the questions stop.
