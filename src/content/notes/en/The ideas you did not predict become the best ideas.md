Ideas you stumble on while linking notes are often better than conclusions you planned your way to. Zettelkasten devices such as links and bottom-up development are, in the end, designed to deliberately provoke these unexpected collisions.

So do not judge in advance whether something will be useful. Always take the note — you cannot know which note will become the raw material for the best idea until it gets connected.
