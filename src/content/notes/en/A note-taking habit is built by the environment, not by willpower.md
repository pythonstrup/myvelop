Human willpower is limited. It can supposedly be trained like a muscle, but it is far more efficient to spend that time reducing the load on the brain instead of trying to grow willpower.

> Ego depletion: a temporary reduction in the self's capacity or willingness to engage in volitional action, caused by prior exercise of volition. — Sönke Ahrens, *How to Take Smart Notes*

The best approach is to minimize what the brain has to decide.
Follow a set pattern for when, where, and what you do. The same routine.

How I apply it:
- Install Obsidian on mobile and set up Sync so I can take notes anytime, anywhere.
- Always save development insights as permanent notes.

Related: [[Follow the eight steps in order]] — "always take notes" is realized not by willpower but by an environment that supports it.
Related: [[Structure beats training]] — the same principle at the organizational level. Guardrails (environment) come before training (willpower).
