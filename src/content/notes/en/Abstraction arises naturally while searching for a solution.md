Great engineers solve problems pragmatically through abstraction.
Making every piece of information concrete in order to understand it costs too much in communication and wastes time.
So abstraction is not a skill to learn in advance but a tool that is naturally demanded in the course of finding a solution.

Related: [[Learning works best when you start from a problem, not from theory]] — just as depth follows the demands of the problem, abstraction arises from the needs of the solving process.
